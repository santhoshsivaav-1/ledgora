The folder this project started from had no `LICENSE` file, which is why the
file was added in Phase 0 (change 0.1).

## Brand and domain: NOT resolved

The MIT licence covers the source code only. It does **not** grant any right to
a trade name, logo or domain.

- The upstream code names itself "ledgora" in `package.json` (`author: ledgora
  <Contact@ledgora.com>`) and links to `ledgora.com`, while the copyright line
  names DiceCodes. Who owns "Ledgora" and `ledgora.com` is therefore unclear.
- Until ownership is confirmed, do not charge customers under the name Ledgora
  and do not point a production domain at it.

### Owner actions before the paid beta (Phase 5)

- [ ] Confirm in writing, from the upstream author, who owns the Ledgora name and ledgora.com, or choose a different product name.
- [ ] Search the trade-mark register for the chosen name and file an application.
- [ ] Register the production domain in the company's name.
- [ ] Have a lawyer review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy drafts (Phase 5.9).
